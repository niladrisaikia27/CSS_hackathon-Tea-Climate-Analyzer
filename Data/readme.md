# Dataset: Kaggle Indian Climate Dataset (2024–2025)

### **Source:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ankushnarwade/indian-climate-dataset-20242025

## Overview

This dataset contains 7,310+ rows of daily climate and air quality data from major Indian cities covering the period January 2024 to December 2025. It is curated for data analysis, visualization, time-series forecasting, and machine learning projects, with clean and well-structured records suitable for beginners and professionals alike.

The dataset captures key environmental indicators such as temperature, rainfall, humidity, wind speed, atmospheric pressure, cloud cover, and Air Quality Index (AQI), enabling in-depth exploration of India’s climate and weather patterns.

## Dataset Features

- Date – Observation date
- City – City name
- State – Indian state
- Temperature_Max (°C) – Daily maximum temperature
- Temperature_Min (°C) – Daily minimum temperature
- Temperature_Avg (°C) – Daily average temperature
- Humidity (%) – Average relative humidity
- Rainfall (mm) – Daily rainfall amount
- Wind_Speed (km/h) – Average wind speed
- AQI (Air Quality Index) – Numerical air quality index
- AQI_Category – Air quality category (Good, Satisfactory, Moderate, Poor, Very Poor, Severe)
- Pressure (hPa) – Atmospheric pressure
- Cloud_Cover (%) – Cloud coverage percentage
